Benedict Westwind¶

Biography¶

Benedict was born in Londinium and grew up in Heister with his younger brother Julian. His parents and brother are all powerful sorcerers and unlike them he's a wizard. He went to School of Evocation with his best friend Kevin in the same year and Julian four years behind him. Even though he had to study hard to get his powers nowadays he's pretty powerful but often he's overshadowed by his younger (more powerful) brother Julian. After he finished school he did some jobs for VOTHD (due to his parents working there) and 2 years later he started his own criminal organisation called "The Cult". The Cult wasn't really popular but he managed to get 5 members - Jack, Dominick, Igor and Elliot (now deceased). They mostly do robberies and drug dealing and they have their own special outfit - black cloaks and white masks.
